================================================================================
FULL CUTOFF SENSITIVITY SWEEP
================================================================================

Method
------
For each (vector_set, layer, cutoff K in {0, 5, 10, ..., 100}):
  1. Iterate training NPZs (train topics 0-79, train trials 0-11, per-
     emotion). For each, take acts[K:].mean(axis=0) as the story's
     mean-residual-at-K. Stories with fewer than 5 tokens remaining are
     excluded from the centroid AND counted as 'short'.
  2. Centroid_E = mean of train-E story-means-at-K.
  3. Contrastive vec_E = centroid_E - mean({centroid_other for other != E}).
  4. Iterate topic-level holdout NPZs (topics 80-99). For each, project
     mean-at-K and assign argmax cosine across the 6 contrastive vectors.

No denoising / no neutral-PC projection-out is applied in this sweep —
the goal is to measure how the contrastive direction's quality varies
with cutoff. (compute_vectors.py applies denoising before saving;
the saved best_layer / holdout JSONs in cutoff_{0,25,50}_ARE built from
denoised vectors. This sweep reports the RAW-contrastive accuracy.)

Cutoffs reported here include all K where the fraction of TRAIN stories
with <5 tokens remaining is <= 10%. Cutoffs above that limit
are marked as DROPPED (low-data regime).
