CUTOFF SENSITIVITY SWEEP — DENOISED VARIANT
====================================================================================

Pipeline (per cell)
-------------------
  1. Build raw contrastive vectors at (layer L, cutoff K) from
     train-topic train-trial story means (positions K..N-1).
  2. Project them orthogonal to the top neutral PCs of the matching
     neutral corpus regime (V1 ← neutral_no_thinking story, V2 ←
     neutral_thinking thought, V3 ← neutral_thinking story).
     PC count per layer = smallest k such that cumulative variance
     explained ≥ 0.50 (matches compute_vectors.py:NEUTRAL_PC_VAR_TARGET).
  3. Classify topic-level holdout (topics 80-99) by argmax cosine.
  4. Drop cutoffs where >10% of training stories have <5 tokens
     remaining after applying K (low-data regime).

Raw and denoised numbers below come from the same training/holdout
file sets (same RNG-free deterministic file globs). The ONLY
difference is the project_out step on the contrastive vectors.

Neutral PC dimensionalities per layer (50%-variance cut):
  V1_no_thinking_full     L13: k=61  L17: k=78  L21: k=43  L25: k=70  L28: k=49  L32: k=43
  V2_thinking_thought     L13: k=36  L17: k=63  L21: k=44  L25: k=33  L28: k=36  L32: k=25
  V3_thinking_reply       L13: k=66  L17: k=81  L21: k=44  L25: k=72  L28: k=54  L32: k=44
