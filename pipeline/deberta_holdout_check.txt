====================================================================================
DeBERTa-v3-large-zeroshot-v2.0 HOLDOUT-CORPUS CHECK
====================================================================================

Same method as pipeline/deberta_corpus_check.txt (deberta-v3-large,
template "This text expresses {}.", multi_label=True), but applied to
the HOLDOUT corpus, sliced by two flavors:
  topic_level   — topics 80-99 (entirely held-out topics)
  within_cell   — topics 0-79, trial-split=holdout (trials 12-14)

For the contrastive comparison, the contrastive vectors were built from
TRAIN-TOPIC TRAIN-TRIAL files at each set's best raw (L, K) cell — i.e.
a true generalization test (the holdout stories did NOT contribute to
the centroids).

Corpus sizes:
  V1_no_thinking_full: topic-level n=1800, within-cell n=1440, total=3240
  V2_thinking_thought: topic-level n=238, within-cell n=470, total=708
  V3_thinking_reply: topic-level n=1785, within-cell n=1410, total=3195
